A 10-person team sends roughly **20,000 emails per year**. That's 20,000 impressions you're either branding — or wasting.

## Email is your highest-frequency channel

Social posts get scheduled. Ads get paused. But every support reply, sales follow-up, and founder intro goes out with whatever signature that person last pasted into Gmail.

For SMBs, employee email often outperforms LinkedIn in raw touchpoints per week.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Think impressions, not campaigns">
  One promotional banner in a shared signature template can outperform a monthly newsletter — because it rides on mail people already send.
</Callout>

## Consistency beats creativity (at this scale)

You don't need a unique signature per employee. You need:

- Correct logo and colors
- Current job title and phone
- One optional CTA block the whole org shares

When marketing launches a webinar or product update, swap the CTA once — not in fifteen individual settings panels.

## Trust signals compound

Branded outbound reinforces legitimacy. Recipients subconsciously note:

- Professional formatting
- Matching domain links
- Authenticated mail ([SPF/DKIM/DMARC](/blog/spf-dkim-dmarc-explained))

A plain-text signature from a `@gmail.com` address sends a different signal than a polished block from `@yourcompany.com` with proper authentication.

## Getting started without IT overhead

1. Pick one [signature template](/blog/email-signature-best-practices)
2. Host logos and icons (don't paste from desktop)
3. Roll out via copy-paste install for Gmail and Outlook
4. Set a quarterly review to update titles, links, and promo blocks
